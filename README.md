# Empirical Studies on Quantum Optimization for Software Engineering: A Systematic Analysis

This repository contains results of 76 primary studies for the study titled _Systematic Analysis of Empirical Studies on Quantum Optimization for Software Engineering_. 

**Notes**:
+ [selected.csv](selected.csv): The 77 primary studies collected by a recent survey paper [Zhang et al., TOSEM, 2026](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3816147).
+ [final.csv](final.csv): The final 76 primary actually being used in our study. Especially, the study *Performance-Driven QUBO for Recommender Systems on Quantum Annealers* was excluded because it had not yet been published as a peer-reviewed work at the time our SLR was finalized.
